01 / The house

Built around
a old garden.

The house was a family home for forty years. We kept the garden, the mango trees, and the long veranda, and turned the rest into twelve rooms that feel like they have always been here.

There is no lobby music and no television in the common rooms. There is a library, a small bar that opens when the first guest asks, and a gardener who will tell you what is flowering if you look interested.

We are close enough to the city for a meeting and far enough for the noise to disappear by the second turn.
