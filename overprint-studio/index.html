PRINTFIRSTALWAYS

01 / The studio

Design you can
hold in your hand.

We believe a brand should survive being printed badly, folded, and left in a bag. If it only works on a screen, it is not finished.

Overprint is a small studio: two designers, a print producer, and a developer. We work from the stock up, choose the ink, and stay until the last page is bound. The web comes after, not instead.

We take on six to eight projects a year. That is enough to do the work properly and little enough to remember every decision.

02 / Services

Four things, done properly.

01

Identity

Names, marks, type systems, and the rules that keep a brand recognisable when somebody else is applying it.

02

Editorial

Books, reports, magazines, and catalogues. Grids that hold their shape, and print production that does not surprise you.

03

Packaging

Structure, dielines, materials, and the small unboxing decisions that make a product feel considered.

04

Web

Sites that carry the same identity without becoming a template. Fast, accessible, and built to be edited.

04 / Process

How a project runs.

01

Read the brief

We ask what the thing is for before we ask what it should look like.

02

Draw on paper

Fast, cheap, and honest. The first ideas should survive a pencil.

03

Proof it

Real stock, real ink, real fold. We do not approve a print from a screen.

04

Hand it over

Files, rules, and a short guide so the next person can keep it consistent.
