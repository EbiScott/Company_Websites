SELECTED WORK

Three recent projects. Names changed where the client asked.

A.01

Ikorodu Workshop

Industrial / 2,400 m² / 2025

A steel-framed workshop with a concrete plinth, a sawtooth roof, and a clear span that keeps the floor entirely free. Built in two phases while the client kept working next door.

A.02

Bourdillon House

Residential / 640 m² / 2024

A family house on a tight plot: a deep veranda, cross ventilation, and a concrete stair that became the only decorative object in the building.

A.03

Yaba Market Hall

Civic / 1,100 m² / 2023

A covered market with a simple zinc roof, open sides, and a drainage strategy that finally keeps the stalls dry in the rainy season.
