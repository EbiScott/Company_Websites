01 / The product

One desk. Four jobs.

Monarch does not try to be a terminal, a broker, or a newsletter. It watches your list and tells you when a rule fires.

01

Watchlist

Your symbols, with delayed quotes and the day's move. No feed, no autoplay video, no comments.

02

Rule alerts

RSI bands, yield thresholds, price levels, and dividend dates. When a rule fires, you get one message.

03

News filter

Only headlines that mention your companies, deduplicated and stripped of the usual noise.

04

Daily digest

Everything that happened, in one short read at the close. No notifications after hours unless you ask.
