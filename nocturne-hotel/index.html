01 / The hotel

Built for the hour
after the meeting.

Nocturne is a small hotel for people who arrive late and leave slowly. Fourteen rooms, one kitchen, a listening room, and a roof with a view that makes the traffic look deliberate.

The building was a merchant house before it was a hotel. We kept the deep corridors and the shutters, added better beds, and made the ground floor somewhere you would actually choose to sit. Nothing here is loud on purpose.
